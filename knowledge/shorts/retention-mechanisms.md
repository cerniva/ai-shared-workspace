# Shorts Retention & Engagement Mechanisms

Status: canonical production input
Updated: 2026-09-29
Rule: evaluate all 40 for each finalist, deliberately select only the best 3–5, and record selected IDs. These are hypotheses/creative mechanisms, not guaranteed causal effects.

1. Immediate visual payoff — show the interesting result/action in frame one.
2. Satisfying / ASMR progression — tactile, rhythmic or visually gratifying progress.
3. Curiosity gap — reveal enough to create a specific unanswered question.
4. Open loop — establish an unresolved outcome that closes near the end.
5. Before → after transformation.
6. Prediction challenge — invite viewer to predict the result.
7. Counterintuitive fact — lead with a verified surprising contrast.
8. Stakes — make the consequence or value clear immediately.
9. Time constraint / countdown.
10. Progress bar / visible completion state.
11. Pattern interruption — purposeful visual/audio change when attention may decay.
12. Fast first cut — remove introductory dead time.
13. Micro-payoffs — deliver small rewards before the final payoff.
14. Escalation — each beat increases novelty, difficulty or consequence.
15. Reveal ladder — disclose information in ordered stages.
16. Demonstration over explanation — show evidence/action instead of only telling.
17. Visual proof — put the claim and its observable evidence together.
18. Specificity — concrete number/object/place instead of vague framing.
19. Contrast — compare A/B, expectation/reality or wrong/right.
20. Motion continuity — preserve directional/action flow between cuts.
21. Match cut / transformation cut.
22. Punch-in / crop change for emphasis.
23. On-screen keyword emphasis — minimal mobile-readable text on the key phrase.
24. Caption rhythm — subtitles chunked to speech/action rather than paragraph blocks.
25. Question hook — a question with a payoff the video actually answers.
26. Direct promise — state exactly what the viewer will learn/see.
27. Mystery object / hidden identity reveal.
28. Ranked choice / best-vs-worst framing when evidence supports ranking.
29. Myth → test → result structure.
30. Problem → attempt → solution structure.
31. Setup → complication → payoff mini-story.
32. Human reaction/emotion when authentic and rights-safe.
33. Viewer participation — choose A/B, predict, count or spot something.
34. Useful save-worthy takeaway — compact procedure, checklist or fact.
35. Comment-worthy bounded question — specific choice/opinion, not engagement bait.
36. Rewatch density — a detail-rich moment worth seeing again without obscuring meaning.
37. Seamless loop — final motion/audio/idea connects naturally to frame one.
38. Callback — final beat resolves/references the opening hook.
39. Audio punctuation — purposeful hit, pause, riser or transition supporting an edit.
40. Sensory sound reward — crunch, sizzle, snap, click, pour or other rights-safe/original Foley aligned to visible action.

## Selection record
For every produced Short store: `mechanisms_evaluated=1-40`, `mechanisms_selected=[...]`, one-line rationale per selected ID, and later owned-channel analytics. Do not promote a mechanism to a permanent lesson from one video.
